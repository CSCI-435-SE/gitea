## Question 2: What is one thing you learned about the codebase or about software engineering practice?

While generally covered in existing material as part of a course on project management I first had to undertake as the final section of my Bachelor's, it is beneficial to have specific coverage of management and good practices that have been created by those that have come before me specifically for the field of digital software design. While the separation of individual elements of software into modules is not unexplored territory, the specific language and division of these concepts into coupling & cohesion is something I have been recently trying to find words to express and explore on other projects. Modular design is useful for human use of course, as it assists in the creation of systems that can be mentally divided and in some aspects expected to work independently of each other, something important for collaborative creation or monolithic systems; but as we progress further into the use of agentic tools for the creation of systems, it is critical: it gives us the ability to deploy multiple agents, reduces the context drift and the costs of feeding them a lot of information, and provides some protection by restricting the impact of a bad change.
